The coach now works on one or two corners at a time and analyzes each pass right after the last corner, instead of waiting for the end of the lap. Cues are placed so that they have time to be spoken, and a small widget window, without title bar or border, stays on top of the game.

**Pre-release**: tested by CI, in replay on a recorded session at Virginia International Raceway (cue placement and per-pass analysis against a Garage61 reference) and with a simulated race engineer. The focus loop has not yet run with Claude on a real session, nor the widget over iRacing.

## Changes since v0.4.0

- **Focus coaching** replaces the planning of the next lap. An analysis takes about 15 s: when it ran at the end of each lap, its cues arrived after the first corners of the next one. Now:
  1. after a complete, valid lap, the race engineer picks one corner, or two consecutive corners, announces them (you can set a save point before them) and places one cue on each;
  2. each pass through these corners is compared to the reference as soon as you leave the last one. The time is spoken at once ("Two tenths off, best yet."), then the race engineer gives the one correction that matters and adjusts the cues while you go around or reset;
  3. it moves on when the gap over these corners is 0.05 s or less, when three clean attempts no longer beat your best, after 12 attempts, or when you ask: say it to the coach ("let's work on something else", "let's do turn three") or click **Next**.
- A pass counts from the middle of the straight before the first focus corner. After a reset, it counts if the car restarts before that point.
- **Cues that have time to be spoken**: the time left before each braking zone is measured on the reference lap (the current speed overestimated it on corner exits, where the car accelerates). The cues of one straight are packed back to back so that all of them end before the braking zone, and a cue that would start too late is moved earlier. A cue that still does not fit is shown in red, logged, and reported to Claude with the longest sentence that fits. Dropped cues are logged.
- **Coaching widget**: a separate 420 × 200 window, without title bar, border or taskbar entry, always on top: coach state, live delta, focus corners, next cue and last sentence spoken. Drag it anywhere on its surface; it reopens where you left it. It replaces the compact pinned window: the main window keeps its size. The **Widget** button opens and closes it, and it opens on its own while iRacing runs (Setup option, on by default; your v0.4.0 "always on top" choice carries over). Clicking the widget takes the keyboard focus, as any window: click back into iRacing after moving it.
- **Coaching screen**: a Focus panel with the corners, the time of each attempt and the **Next** button.
- **MCP**: new tools `set_focus` and `next_focus`; `update_plan` returns the speaking time each cue needs and has, with a warning for each cue that does not fit; `get_session_overview` includes the focus state. The `[Context]` block of each driver message includes the focus corners and the latest attempts.
- **Prompts**: the coach and race engineer prompts describe the focus loop. A session `CLAUDE.md` left unedited by v0.2.0 to v0.4.0 is replaced by the new default at startup; an edited one is kept, and the new default is written next to it as `CLAUDE.default.md`.
- **Building**: Rust 1.93 or newer.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --replay "session.ibt" --reference "Garage 61 - ... .csv"
                                     replay of a recorded session against a Garage61 lap
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Export the reference lap from garage61.net as a CSV and load it with **Load lap…** on the Coaching screen, or with `--reference`. Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button and the audio devices. Focus coaching starts after the first complete, valid lap.
