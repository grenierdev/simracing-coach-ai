The coach now sees your line: path radius, steering angle, turn-in point and how your line departs from the reference in each corner. The coaching window can also pin itself on top while iRacing runs.

**Pre-release**: tested in synthetic replay and by CI; not yet run on a real PC with iRacing, a wheel and a microphone.

## Changes since v0.3.0

- **Line analysis**, per corner, against the reference:
  - **turn-in point** (where the steering reaches 30 % of the corner's peak), in meters earlier or later;
  - **path radius** (speed ÷ yaw rate): the tightest radius, where it is (geometric apex), and the median radius in the core of the corner;
  - **steering angle**, peak and mean;
  - **line offset** at the reference's turn-in, apex and exit, in meters towards the inside or the outside.
- **Your line in meters**: from GPS when available (.ibt, Garage61 references), otherwise rebuilt from speed and heading (iRacing's `Yaw`, now read from the telemetry). Live, against a Garage61 reference, your line is aligned on the reference over the straight before each corner: the offset shows how your line departs from it after a common approach.
- **Coach and race engineer prompts**: a tighter radius with more steering and less rotation means the line is too tight; the advice is to open the entry and take a later apex, not to turn in earlier. Differences under 10 % or 1 m are treated as noise.
- **New MCP data**: `get_corner_summary` and the session overview include the new values; `get_corner_trace` gains the `radius` and `line_offset` channels.
- **Chat screen**: the corner table shows Turn-in, Radius, Steer and Line columns.
- **Pin on top with iRacing** (Windows, on by default): the coaching window pins itself when the live iRacing telemetry connects and is released, with its previous size restored, when iRacing stops. Pinning or unpinning by hand takes over.
- **Prompt upgrades**: a session `CLAUDE.md` left unedited by an earlier version is replaced by the new default at startup; an edited one is kept, and the new default is written next to it as `CLAUDE.default.md`.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button and the audio devices. To check the line analysis, look at the Turn-in, Radius, Steer and Line columns on the **Chat** screen after a few laps.
