You can mix French and English when you speak: the coach and the race engineer reply and write their cues only in the language chosen in Setup, the language of the voice.

**Pre-release**: tested by CI and in replay with a simulated race engineer. A real iRacing reset and the race engineer running with Claude on a real session have not been tested yet.

## Changes since v0.8.1

- **Language repeated in every message**: each message sent to the coach and each batch of turns sent to the race engineer says to write only in the voice language, whatever language you speak. A cue in place in the other language is marked to be rewritten.
- **Stronger instructions**: both sessions are told that the voice can only pronounce its own language, so they must keep to it even when you mix languages.
- **Safety net**: `update_plan` and `say` refuse a sentence clearly written in the other language, and Claude rewrites it before anything is spoken. A short or mixed sentence the program is not sure about is accepted. English racing jargon used in French ("trail braking", "apex") is fine.

The language is the one active at launch: a change in Setup applies at the next start, and `--language` overrides the setting. Speech recognition has not been tested on sentences that mix both languages.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --replay "session.ibt" --reference "Garage 61 - ... .csv"
                                     replay of a recorded session against a Garage61 lap
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Export the reference lap from garage61.net as a CSV and load it with **Load lap…** on the Coaching screen, or with `--reference`. Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button, the audio devices, the volume and the voice language. Once a reference lap is loaded, every turn you drive is analyzed; click **Widget** to open the widget, or let it open with iRacing.
