In focus coaching, the race engineer's advice is now spoken as soon as each pass is analyzed, which is also your cue to reset and try again. The coaching widget is down to four lines, shows where time can be gained, can be resized and has no shadow. The coach's voice has a volume setting.

**Pre-release**: tested by CI and in replay with a simulated race engineer. The widget's window style (no shadow) and its resize grip are Windows code that has not yet run on a real PC; the focus loop has not yet run with Claude on a real session.

## Changes since v0.5.0

- **Advice right after each analysis**: after each pass through the focus corners, the time is still spoken at once; then the race engineer's reply, the one correction for the next attempt, is spoken as soon as it arrives, instead of a cue placed before the corner. It tells you to reset and go again. The announcement of new focus corners, with the first thing to change, is also spoken at once. No cue waits before the corners during focus coaching.
- **Coaching widget**:
  - four lines, 380 × 92 by default: the coach state, the focus corners and number of tries, the time to gain, the next cue and the last sentence spoken;
  - one state at a time (*Listening*, *Speaking*, *Thinking*, *Analyzing* or *Idle*);
  - no live delta; the **Gain** line, highlighted, shows the time still to gain on the focus corners and your best try ("0.21 s on T5, T6 (best 0.15)"), or, outside focus coaching, the three corners where the last lap lost the most;
  - resizable by the grip in its bottom-right corner; it reopens where and as you left it;
  - no drop shadow or rounded corners: the window is turned into a plain popup once created.
- **Coach volume** (Setup → Voice): 0 to 150 %, from the next sentence, saved in the settings. Click the bar for 10 % steps, − and + for 5 %.
- **Prompts**: the race engineer prompt follows the new loop. A session `CLAUDE.md` left unedited by v0.2.0 to v0.5.0 is replaced by the new default at startup; an edited one is kept, and the new default is written next to it as `CLAUDE.default.md`.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --replay "session.ibt" --reference "Garage 61 - ... .csv"
                                     replay of a recorded session against a Garage61 lap
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Export the reference lap from garage61.net as a CSV and load it with **Load lap…** on the Coaching screen, or with `--reference`. Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button, the audio devices and the volume. Focus coaching starts after the first complete, valid lap; click **Widget** to open the widget, or let it open with iRacing.
