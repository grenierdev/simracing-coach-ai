Every turn is now analyzed as soon as you leave it, and its cue is updated for the next pass. Drive full laps and hear every turn's cue, or reset to a save point and drill one turn or a sequence of turns. Cues are spoken before the turn whether you are braking or not, and a reset no longer silences them. The interface and the widget are simpler, with a new dark theme.

**Pre-release**: tested by CI and in replay with a simulated race engineer. A real iRacing reset and the race engineer running with Claude on a real session have not been tested yet.

## Changes since v0.6.0

- **Analysis after every turn**: each turn is compared to the reference as soon as the car leaves it, then joins a queue that keeps only its latest pass. When the race engineer is free, it gets up to four queued turns at a time and updates their cues (`auto-T5`). Each cue is spoken before its turn on the next pass.
- **Drills by reset**: after a reset to a save point before a turn, that turn's cue is spoken at once. The pass counts from where the car reappears, if that is before the braking point. Its time is spoken as soon as you leave the turn ("Turn 4. Two tenths off, best yet."), then the cue is updated.
- **Focus coaching removed**: no more focus corners, **Next** button, `set_focus` or `next_focus` tools. Where you reset decides what you work on.
- **Cues before the turn, regardless of braking**: braking no longer holds back or cuts off a cue; cues are still placed to end before the braking point. The former rule (no speech while braking or in quiet zones) is available with `silence_in_braking = true` in a plan's `[settings]`.
- **Resets**: a cue is spoken once per pass instead of once per lap number, so it plays again after a reset within the same lap. A sentence waiting to be spoken is no longer lost when the race engineer updates a cue.
- **Interface**: near-black background, cyan accents, square corners, Bahnschrift font. The Coaching screen has one header line (state, reference, lap, live delta), the turns table (last pass, best, cue; ◆ being analyzed, ◇ queued), the next cue, the last sentence spoken and the race engineer's last summary. The level meters moved to Setup.
- **Coaching widget**: three lines, 380 × 72 by default: the state with the turns that have the most time to gain, the next cue, the last sentence spoken. A bar on its left edge takes the color of the state. It stays resizable.
- **Session overview**: the state of each turn (passes, last, best, queued or being analyzed) is in the conversation's `[Context]` block and in `get_session_overview`.
- **Prompts**: both prompts follow the new loop. A session `CLAUDE.md` left unedited from an earlier version is replaced by the new default at startup; an edited one is kept, and the new default is written next to it as `CLAUDE.default.md`.

**Cost**: on full laps, this uses more of the subscription quota than one analysis per lap, since every turn is analyzed (up to four per request). Turn the analysis off on the Setup screen, or start with `--no-planning`, to stop it.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --replay "session.ibt" --reference "Garage 61 - ... .csv"
                                     replay of a recorded session against a Garage61 lap
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Export the reference lap from garage61.net as a CSV and load it with **Load lap…** on the Coaching screen, or with `--reference`. Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button, the audio devices and the volume. Once a reference lap is loaded, every turn you drive is analyzed; click **Widget** to open the widget, or let it open with iRacing.
