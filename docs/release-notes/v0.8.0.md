The race engineer's cues are now spoken only on the approach to their turn, never when the analysis arrives. This works the same while hot-lapping and while drilling a corner with active reset.

**Pre-release**: tested by CI and in replay with a simulated race engineer. A real iRacing reset and the race engineer running with Claude on a real session have not been tested yet.

## Changes since v0.7.0

- **No speech from the race engineer on arrival**: the race engineer's session no longer has the `say` tool, which spoke a sentence right away or on the next straight. It can only update the cues, and the program speaks each cue on the approach to its turn. The conversation coach keeps `say` for your spoken requests.
- **Cue rewritten while it waits**: after a reset, a cue can be armed but still waiting, for example while your time is being spoken. If the race engineer rewrites it then, it keeps its place and the new text is spoken, once. Before, the plan update dropped it and nothing was said on that attempt.
- **Cue rewritten after its approach**: nothing is said when the update arrives; the new text is spoken on the next pass.

What you hear:

- **hot laps**: each cue at its place before the braking point, updated from your last pass through the turn;
- **active reset before a turn**: if the car reappears before the cue's place, the cue is spoken there; if it reappears between that place and the braking point, the cue is spoken at once. Your time is spoken after the turn;
- **reset before the analysis is back** (about 15 s): the previous cue on that attempt, the new one on the next.

## Contents

`simcoach.exe` is a standalone executable: C runtime, sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime statically linked, no DLL to install. The archive also contains `plans/synthetic.toml` (example plan) and the README.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64
- The Claude CLI logged in once with the subscription: `claude`, then `/login`
- The ACP adapter: `claude-agent-acp.exe` on the PATH, or Node.js (the program then runs `npx -y @agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp@0.107.0`)
- Internet access on first launch: the voice of the selected language (about 80 MB) and speech recognition (about 670 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\simcoach\data\models`

## Getting started

```
simcoach.exe                         live iRacing telemetry
simcoach.exe --language fr           French voice and replies
simcoach.exe --replay "session.ibt" --reference "Garage 61 - ... .csv"
                                     replay of a recorded session against a Garage61 lap
simcoach.exe --synthetic             demo without iRacing
simcoach.exe --help
```

Export the reference lap from garage61.net as a CSV and load it with **Load lap…** on the Coaching screen, or with `--reference`. Open **Setup** to set the push-to-talk button, the audio devices and the volume. Once a reference lap is loaded, every turn you drive is analyzed; click **Widget** to open the widget, or let it open with iRacing.
